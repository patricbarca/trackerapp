Performance ratio calculator

A quick PR check using the standard definition: actual AC energy divided by the energy the DC capacity would produce at STC for the measured plane-of-array irradiation.

PR = EAC ÷ ( PDC,STC × HPOA ÷ 1 kW/m² )

Temperature-corrected PR divides by a correction factor 1 + γ·(Tcell − 25 °C). This is a simplified single-period estimate; contractual PR usually applies data filters and time-step (e.g. 15-minute) corrections defined in the EPC or O&M contract.
